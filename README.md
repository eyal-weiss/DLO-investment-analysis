## Thesis Summary

**Date:** March 2026 | **Data basis:** FY2024 audited financials, 9M 2025 results

DLocal operates as critical payments infrastructure in the world's fastest-growing consumer markets. The company processes ~$38B in TPV (2025E) across 40+ countries through a single-API platform, generating ~$1.05B in revenue with 26% Adjusted EBITDA margins.

### Key Investment Pillars

1. **TPV growth** driven by merchant expansion and geographic penetration in underpenetrated emerging markets
2. **Net take rate** compresses gradually but is more than offset by volume growth
3. **Operating leverage** in a platform business with a debt-free balance sheet generating substantial free cash flow

## Methodology

- 5-year DCF with Gordon Growth terminal value (WACC 11.5%, terminal growth 4.0%)
- Bottom-up operating model anchored in TPV growth, net take rate dynamics, and geographic expansion
- Cross-checked with EV/EBITDA and P/E exit multiple valuations
- Scenario analysis across bull, base, and bear cases
